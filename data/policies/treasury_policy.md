# Corporate Treasury Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy governs the management of corporate cash, liquidity, and short-term investments for all operating entities. It applies to all treasury operations across USD, EUR, and GBP currency holdings.

## 2. Minimum Reserve Requirements

### 2.1 Operating Reserves
Each currency must maintain minimum operating reserves equal to 100% of the next 14 days' projected outflows.

### 2.2 Strategic Reserves
A strategic reserve of $2,000,000 USD equivalent must be maintained across all currencies at all times.

### 2.3 Reserve Monitoring
Cash position must be reviewed daily. Any projected breach of minimum reserves within a 30-day horizon must be escalated to the VP of Treasury immediately.

## 3. Investment of Surplus Funds

### 3.1 Surplus Definition
Surplus is defined as cash balances exceeding 120% of the next 30 days' projected obligations for a given currency.

### 3.2 Investment Requirements
Surplus funds exceeding the 120% threshold should be invested in approved short-term instruments to maximize yield while maintaining liquidity.

### 3.3 Approved Instruments
- Bank term deposits (up to 90 days)
- Government treasury bills
- Investment-grade commercial paper (A1/P1 rated minimum)
- Money market funds (AAA rated)

### 3.4 Maximum Single Investment
No single investment shall exceed 40% of total surplus for the relevant currency.

### 3.5 Maximum Term
No investment shall have a maturity exceeding 90 days without CFO approval.

## 4. Bank Relationship Management

### 4.1 Counterparty Limits
No more than 60% of total cash may be held with any single banking counterparty.

### 4.2 Approved Banks
- Chase (USD primary)
- Bank of America (USD secondary)
- Deutsche Bank (EUR primary)
- BNP Paribas (EUR secondary)
- Barclays (GBP primary)

## 5. Intercompany Transfers

### 5.1 Authorization
Intercompany transfers exceeding $1,000,000 USD equivalent require VP Treasury approval.

### 5.2 Documentation
All intercompany transfers must be documented with business justification and posted to SAP within the same business day.

## 6. Credit Facilities

### 6.1 Usage Guidelines
Credit facility drawdowns are a measure of last resort, to be used only when:
- Projected shortfalls cannot be covered by intercompany transfers
- Collections acceleration is insufficient
- Investment maturities cannot be aligned

### 6.2 Maximum Utilization
Total credit facility utilization shall not exceed 50% of committed lines at any time.
